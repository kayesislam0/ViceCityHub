GTA 6 map: the six regions of Leonida

Rockstar has named six parts of Leonida, its take on Florida.

  • Vice City

    The Miami-inspired city: beaches, nightlife, Ocean Drive.

  • Leonida Keys

    An island chain after the Florida Keys. Beautiful and risky water.

  • Grassrivers

    Mangrove wetlands after the Everglades, with Vice City's skyline in sight.

  • Port Gellhorn

    The forgotten coast: a faded resort town of cheap motels and dead attractions.

  • Ambrosia

    The industrial heart of the state, built around a sugar refinery.

  • Mount Kalaga National Park

    Forests and trails against the northern border. Hunting, fishing, wildlife.

Questions

How do you enter cheats in GTA 6?

Finish the main story to unlock the Cheats list in the Progress menu. Then press Up on the D-pad to open the phone, hold L1 + R1 on PS5 or LB + RB on Xbox, and enter the code on the D-pad.

Do GTA 6 cheats disable trophies and saving?

Yes. While a cheat is on you can't save the game, and trophies and achievements are switched off.

What is the GTA 6 money cheat?

$250,000: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. It unlocks when you finish the main story.

How do I track my GTA 6 map progress?

Tap each region of Leonida on the map as you go: passed through, explored, or done 100%. Your percentage and rank update straight away and stay saved in your browser, so you can come back after every session.

What is Leonida Wrapped?

A recap card of your whole launch run: how much of Leonida you saw, your final rank, your best Leonida Daily streak and the regions you finished. Pick it under Card, then share or download it.

What time does GTA 6 unlock?

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, 2026, starting in New Zealand and ending in Mexico and Central America. On the PlayStation Store the USA and Canada share one unlock at midnight Eastern, so the US West Coast can play from 9 PM Pacific on November 18. The UK and Ireland unlock at midnight GMT and Mainland Europe at midnight CET. Your account region decides your unlock time.

What time does GTA 6 unlock in the UK and Europe?

On the PlayStation Store, the UK and Ireland unlock at midnight GMT on November 19, 2026, and Mainland Europe at midnight CET, which is 11 PM GMT on November 18.

What time does GTA 6 unlock in Australia?

Midnight AEDT on November 19, 2026, which is 1 PM UTC on November 18. Australia is one of the first regions to unlock, after New Zealand.

Can I play GTA 6 early by changing my console clock?

No. Your unlock follows your PlayStation or Xbox account region, not the clock on your console.

What are the regions of Leonida?

Rockstar has named six: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.
