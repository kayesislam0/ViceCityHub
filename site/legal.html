Using the hub

The hub is provided as it is, for fun, without any warranty. Game facts can change and may be incomplete. Don't use the hub, its links or its cards to harass anyone, to impersonate anyone, or to break the law. The code is open source under the MIT licence; see the project on GitHub.

Tips

Tips are voluntary gifts to the person who runs the hub. They are not a purchase, a donation to a charity or an investment, and they buy nothing: no in-game money, items, perks or features. Every tool stays free whether you tip or not. Crypto payments can't be reversed, so tips can't be refunded. Always check the address in your wallet before you send, and only trust the tip details shown on this site: we never ask for crypto by message. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before sending anything. You are responsible for any taxes or rules that apply to you.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers.
  • Your map, checklists, settings, streak and bingo board are saved only in your own browser's local storage. They never reach us. Clear them with "Clear my map" or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Cards are drawn on your device. Nothing you make is uploaded.
  • A challenge link contains your map progress and the name you typed, because that is what your friend sees. Don't type anything personal into the name field.
  • The site is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes basic request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site. We don't receive or store it ourselves.
  • If you tip, your payment goes through Binance or the Bitcoin network under their own terms. Bitcoin transactions are public by design. We don't collect anything else about you.

Rights holders and contact

If you believe something on the hub infringes your rights, or for anything else, contact the maintainer through the project's GitHub page (use a private report for anything sensitive). We will look at it promptly and remove material where appropriate.
