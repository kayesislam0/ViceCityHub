Week of 19 November 2026 · checked 20 November

  • GTA 6 came out on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It's single-player; Rockstar hasn't announced an online mode or a PC date.
  • It targets 30fps on every console, with no 60fps mode. Some base PS5 players report drops to 15–20fps (PlayStation LifeStyle).

How saving works

  • Autosaves happen after missions and big moments. They sit in a different menu from manual saves.
  • Manual saves have 15 slots. You can't save during a mission.
  • Cash you carry is lost if you're wasted or arrested. Put it in an ATM; the banking app on your phone finds the nearest one.
  • Cheats switch off saving for the rest of that session. Save first, then cheat.
  • Crashes have cost some players hours. Save manually to a new slot before long missions.

Source: TechRadar launch-day coverage.

Known issues and settings tips

Reported by players

  • Crashes on PS5 and Xbox, some during the prologue. On PS5 some see error CE-108255-1.
  • An early Lucia mission where the cutscene doesn't start, blocking progress.
  • Falling through the ground, clipping, and lost saves.
  • Frame drops and input delay in combat, mostly on base PS5.

Community tips (not official)

  • Turn motion blur down if fast driving looks smeary at 30fps.
  • Run the HDR calibration, and lower the stick dead zone if aiming feels sluggish.
  • PS5 stutter: one player fixed it by clearing the system cache and turning off HDCP. Turning off HDCP stops streaming apps and Blu-ray until you turn it back on.

Still stuck? Rockstar Support is the official place to report bugs.

Sources: PCGH, PlayStation LifeStyle, TechRadar.

Money by chapter

Chapter 1
Spend it. Cash is wiped when the chapter ends.
Chapter 2
Sell cars without trackers to Moses ($1,000+).
Chapter 3
Jewelry stores ($10k–25k). Cars with standard trackers at Pay 'n' Spray (about $4k). Supercars from Randy's wish list (about $30k each). $120k is paid automatically when the chapter ends.
Chapter 4
Bank vaults pay up to $205k each, with an instant 4-star wanted level. Yasmani's van tips cost $10k.
After the story
Repeat vault robberies: about $100k–200k every five minutes or so. Or enter the $250,000 cheat, with saving switched off for that session.

Source: PowerPyx, published 18 November 2026.

Getting around

  • Lost the next mission? The Map screen has a mission list. Hold ↓ on the D-pad to pick nearby objectives.
  • RydeMe on your phone is fast travel; Scooter Bros. delivers a scooter.
  • Stole a car you like? Register it at a Pay 'N' Spray to keep it.

Source: TechRadar.
